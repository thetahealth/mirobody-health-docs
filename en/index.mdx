## What is Mirobody?

Mirobody brings reports, device data, photos and genetic files into one record, puts recognized names and units into a shared language so the record can be compared over time, and lets you ask questions with references to the original sources. Standardize enough of your data, for long enough, and you have a complete, continuous account of one body: your Mirobody. This open-source edition does it on your own infrastructure under the [Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/thetahealth/mirobody). Built by Theta Health, it is the health data engine behind Theta.

## Use it without running anything

Try Mirobody in [Theta Web](https://dr.thetahealth.ai/?utm_source=mirobody&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=mirobody-to-theta&utm_content=docs_home), or in the Theta mobile app, to keep your own records without deploying anything. You can also try the [live demo](https://chat.mirobody.ai/demo?ref=mirobody-docs&lang=en) with synthetic records; no account is required.

Find source code, releases and support on [GitHub](https://github.com/thetahealth/mirobody).
