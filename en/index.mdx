## What is Mirobody?

Mirobody is an open-source health-data engine. It brings reports, device data, photos and genetic files into a shared record, standardizes recognized names and units, and lets you ask questions with references to the original sources. Built by Theta Health, it powers the Theta Wellness app and is available under the [Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/thetahealth/mirobody).

## Use it without running anything

Use [Theta](https://dr.thetahealth.ai/en/login?utm_source=mirobody&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=mirobody-to-theta&utm_content=docs_home) on the web or in its mobile app to manage your own records without deploying Mirobody. You can also try the [live demo](https://chat.mirobody.ai/demo?ref=mirobody-docs&lang=en) with synthetic records; no account is required.

Find source code, releases and support on [GitHub](https://github.com/thetahealth/mirobody).
